## Overview

The purpose of this block diagram is to show the overall electrical layout of my subsystem and how the different components will interact. It shows the power levels used throughout the system, the optical slot sensor and op-amp circuit used for sensing, and the motor and motor driver circuits used as actuators. The diagram also shows how these components connect to the PIC18F57Q43 Curiosity Nano and which microcontroller pins and peripherals are being used. Finally, it shows the ribbon cable connections that allow my subsystem to communicate with the other team members' boards and the power sources needed to operate the system.
